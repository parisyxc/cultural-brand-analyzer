# Example: Notion

## Purpose
Validate Ideological Innovation
in productivity and organization tools.

## How to Run (No API)

1. Activate the Cultural Strategy Lab skill

2. Then prompt Claude with:

"Analyze Notion using Cultural Strategy Lab.
Identify the cultural tension around work and order,
and explain how Notion proposes a new ideology of self-organization."

## Expected Archetype
Ideological Innovation

## What to Look For
- New moral framing of productivity
- Tool presented as worldview, not feature set
- Productivity discussed as identity and discipline
