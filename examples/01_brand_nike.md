# Example: Nike

## Purpose
Validate the Ideological Innovation archetype
for identity-driven performance brands.

## How to Run (No API)

1. Activate the Cultural Strategy Lab skill
   (paste ACTIVATE.md into Claude)

2. Then prompt Claude with:

"Analyze Nike using Cultural Strategy Lab.
Identify the cultural tension, ideological vacuum,
and explain why Nike represents an Ideological Innovation archetype."

## Expected Archetype
Ideological Innovation

## What to Look For
- Moral framing of effort, dignity, and struggle
- Identity construction through performance
- Minimal discussion of product features
- Cultural tension articulated before solution
