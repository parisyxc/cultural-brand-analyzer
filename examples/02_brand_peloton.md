# Example: Peloton

## Purpose
Test the boundary between Product as Myth
and Ideological Innovation in fitness brands.

## How to Run (No API)

1. Activate the Cultural Strategy Lab skill

2. Then prompt Claude with:

"Analyze Peloton using Cultural Strategy Lab.
Focus on ritual, identity completion,
and explain why Peloton functions primarily as a Product as Myth."

## Expected Archetype
Product as Myth (primary)
Ideological Innovation (secondary, optional)

## What to Look For
- Product framed as identity completion
- Ritualized daily usage and repetition
- Symbolism outweighs technical performance
