# Case Studies｜案例分析

Full worked analyses produced by this skill, on **real, publicly observable brands**.

> **These are interpretive cultural analyses, not client work.** Each was produced by reading a
> brand's public presence (website, Instagram, product language) through Douglas Holt's Cultural
> Innovation Theory. They make no claim about any company's internal strategy, intentions, or
> business results — and no brand here is or was a client.

## What's here

| Case | Primary archetype | Why it's a useful reference |
|------|------------------|-----------------------------|
| **[Smythson](Smythson/smythson_cultural_analysis.md)** | Mythic Corporation | Meaning generated by *institutional continuity* — 1887, unchanged craft, New Bond Street. Shows how to read a brand whose message is what it refuses to change. Ships with a separate [archetype routing decision](Smythson/archetype_routing_decision.md) documenting *why* this archetype and not the others. |
| **[Rapha](rapha/rapha_cultural_analysis.md)** | Cultural Capital Commodification | Meaning generated by *teaching taste* — clubhouses, group rides, insider language as a cultural learning system. Shows how to read a brand that sells cultural upgrading. |

## How to use them

These serve three purposes:

1. **Output reference** — what a finished analysis looks like, end to end.
2. **Archetype calibration** — two archetypes worked in depth (`Mythic Corporation`, `Cultural Capital Commodification`), complementing the `Ideological Innovation` cases in `../context_documents/`.
3. **Routing discipline** — the Smythson routing-decision file is the clearest demonstration of the "why *not* the other archetypes" reasoning the skill requires. Read it before routing a hard case.

The difference from `../examples/`: those are short *reasoning-stability checks* you can re-run to verify the skill still behaves. These are *full analyses* — longer, deeper, and closer to a real deliverable's shape.
