# Case: Nike – Recrafting the American Dream

This case illustrates how a brand achieved breakthrough status
by innovating at the ideological level rather than the product level.

---

## Cultural Tension

By the late 1970s and 1980s, the traditional American Dream
— upward mobility through hard work —
was no longer delivering security or dignity.

Ordinary people experienced:
- Economic stagnation
- Loss of manufacturing jobs
- Erosion of masculine and working-class identity
- Growing distance from elite success narratives

Effort no longer guaranteed meaning or reward.

---

## Historical & Social Context

- Deindustrialization and globalization reshaped labor
- Middle-class stability weakened
- Cultural authority shifted away from institutions
- Athletic achievement remained one of the few credible merit systems

Sport became a symbolic arena for restoring personal agency.

---

## Ideological Vacuum

The dominant ideology of success based on status, credentials,
and institutional advancement lost legitimacy.

People lacked a believable framework for:
- Dignity without elite status
- Heroism without privilege
- Meaning without guaranteed outcomes

---

## Ideological Myth

Nike introduced a new ideology:
self-made heroism through discipline and action.

Key elements:
- Greatness is built, not given
- Training equals moral strength
- Action creates meaning, regardless of outcome

The slogan "Just Do It" functioned as a moral directive,
not a motivational tagline.

---

## Cultural Authority

Nike drew authority from:
- Everyday athletes rather than champions
- Working-class discipline and grit
- Urban and marginalized communities
- Counter-elitist interpretations of sport

These sources made the myth feel authentic and democratic.

---

## Rituals & Symbols

- Training as a daily ritual of self-construction
- Sweat, repetition, and endurance as moral signals
- Athletic apparel as tools of personal transformation
- Advertising narratives focused on effort, not victory

Participation mattered more than winning.

---

## Key Takeaway for Analysis

Nike demonstrates that iconic brands emerge
by resolving identity crises through ideology.

Product innovation supports the myth,
but cultural meaning drives long-term brand power.
