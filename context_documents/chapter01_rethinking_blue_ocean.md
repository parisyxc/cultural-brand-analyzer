# Chapter 1 Positioning: Rethinking Blue Ocean Strategy

This document establishes the analytical stance
used throughout all cultural innovation analyses.

---

## Core Rejection

Market-based innovation frameworks (e.g. Blue Ocean Strategy)
are insufficient to explain how iconic brands emerge.

Product differentiation and category creation
do not generate durable cultural meaning.

---

## Core Assertion

Breakthrough brands succeed by:
- Identifying unresolved cultural tensions
- Innovating at the ideological level
- Providing symbolic solutions to social anxiety

---

## Required Analytical Shift

All analyses must:
- Prioritize cultural contradiction over market gaps
- Treat identity and meaning as primary drivers
- Assume functional advantages are easily replicated

---

## Implications for Analysis

When analyzing any brand or technology:
- Do not start from product features
- Do not assume unmet demand equals opportunity
- Always locate the cultural tension first

---

## Non-Negotiable Principle

Cultural innovation is not a campaign or positioning tactic.
It is a long-term process of ideological production.
